A complete financial and architectural breakdown for deploying an AI outbound agent designed to hunt leads, qualify prospects, and route live transfers directly to our 2-person closing floor with zero manual work.

Phase 1: Automated Lead Generation Engine

Before the AI dials, it needs verified direct-dial mobile numbers. We automate this using a "Waterfall" approach to keep data costs low while maximizing hit rates.

1. Fixed Infrastructure

Monthly SaaS to keep the engine hunting 24/7.

  • Apollo.io (Targeting) ~$100/mo
  • Clay.com (Orchestration) ~$250/mo
  • Make.com (Routing) ~$20/mo
Total Fixed: ~$370/mo

2. Variable "Waterfall"

Cost to find a verified mobile decision-maker number.

  • Tier 1 (Apollo API) ~$0.03/hit
  • Tier 2 (Prospeo) ~$0.12/hit
  • Tier 3 (ContactOut) ~$0.30/hit
Blended Average: ~$0.15/lead

3. Active Line Validation

Ensuring the line is active to save AI compute time.

  • Twilio Carrier Lookup $0.005/hit
  • Filters out VOIP Included
  • Filters out dead lines Included
Validation Cost: $0.005/lead

Total Cost Per Ready-to-Dial Lead

Amortized fixed costs ($0.17) + Waterfall ($0.15) + Validation ($0.005) at 2k leads/mo.

$0.32
